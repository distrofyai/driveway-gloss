Our Story

Detailing done the right way — every single time.

Driveway Gloss started with a simple idea: give people a detail they can actually trust. No rotating crews, no rushed production line, no corners cut when nobody's looking. Just one detailer — Tyler — who treats every car like it's his own.

With over five years of hands-on experience, Tyler personally handles every job from start to finish. Interior deep cleans, exterior hand washes, paint correction, and protective coatings are all done with care, precision, and zero shortcuts — the same standard on a daily driver as on a show car.

And because Driveway Gloss is fully mobile, there's no shop to drive to and no lobby to wait in. Tyler brings the full setup — water and power included — right to your home or office, and leaves your car looking better than the day you got it.
